# Project Title
E-Commerce Sales, Customer & Product Analytics Dashboard

# Short Description / Purpose

Developed a comprehensive Power BI dashboard to analyze e-commerce sales performance, customer behavior, product performance, revenue trends, customer retention, and purchasing patterns. The dashboard helps businesses monitor key KPIs, identify growth opportunities, and make data-driven decisions.

# Tech Stack
Power BI Desktop
Power Query
DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)
Data Modeling
Interactive Visualizations
Slicers & Filters

# Data Source
E-Commerce Transaction Data
Customer Information Data
Product Sales Data
Order & Revenue Records
Country-wise Sales Data
Product Return Data

# Features / Highlights
Executive Overview of business performance
Customer Analytics and Retention Analysis
Product Performance Monitoring
Revenue Forecasting and Trend Analysis
Geographic Revenue Analysis
Customer Lifetime Value (CLV) Analysis
Repeat Purchase Analysis
Interactive filters for Country, Year, Month, and Product Description
Dynamic KPI Cards and Drill-Down Reports

# Business Problem

E-commerce companies generate large volumes of sales and customer data, making it difficult to track performance, understand customer purchasing behavior, identify top-performing products, and improve customer retention. This dashboard centralizes key business metrics to support strategic decision-making and business growth.

# Example Insights

## Sales Insights
Generated over $8.3 Million in total revenue.
Revenue peaked during mid-year and November sales periods.
Certain countries contributed significantly higher revenue than others.

## Customer Insights
High repeat purchase rate indicates strong customer loyalty.
Top customers contributed a large portion of overall revenue.
Customer Lifetime Value fluctuated throughout the year, highlighting changing purchasing behavior.

## Product Insights
A small group of products generated the majority of sales revenue.
Product return analysis helped identify products requiring quality improvements.
Product performance varied across countries and seasons.

## Forecasting Insights
Predicted revenue trends closely followed historical sales patterns.
Forecasting can support future inventory and sales planning.

# Key Questions Solved
### Business Performance
1. What is the total revenue generated?
2. How many customers are active?
3. Which countries contribute the highest revenue?
### Customer Analytics
1. What is the average customer lifetime value?
2. How many customers make repeat purchases?
3. Who are the top customers by revenue?
### Product Analytics
1. Which products generate the highest revenue?
2. Which products have the highest return rates?
3. Which product categories perform best?
### Advanced Analytics
1. What are the seasonal purchasing patterns?
2. How is customer lifetime value changing over time?
